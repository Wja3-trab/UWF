We go beyond short-term aid. At Ummah Works Foundation, we support orphans by strengthening the communities around them, through education, local projects, and longterm opportunity.

What we believe

We believe every orphan deserves more than survival.
They deserve a chance to grow, learn, and lead. All while being within a strong, self-reliant community. That’s why we invest in sustainable projects like schools, farms, and local businesses, giving children the tools and environment to thrive.

What we do

  • Child-Centered Support – Care, education, and mentorship for orphans.
  • Community Development – Schools, training, and local infrastructure.
  • Sustainable Projects – Farming, entrepreneurship, and small businesses.
  • Local Partnerships – We work with communities, not for them
