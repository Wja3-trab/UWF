What we do

Ummah Works Foundation helps orphans by building strong, supportive communities around them. We focus on lasting change, not quick fixes.

Putting Children First

We care for orphaned children by making sure they have a safe place to live, access to education, and someone to guide them. Every child deserves to grow up feeling secure, valued, and hopeful about the future.

Strengthening Communities

We build and support schools, skill centers, and safe spaces where children can learn, grow, and build a better life together. When the community grows stronger, so does the child.

Creating Sustainable Projects

We support local farms, small businesses, and training programs that help communities become more independent. These projects provide food, jobs, and long-term support for orphans and their neighbors.

Working with Local People

We don’t bring outside solutions. We listen and work with local families, leaders, and volunteers to create change that really fits their needs.
