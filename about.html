About Us

Who are we?

At Ummah Works Foundation, we believe in building lasting impact through action. We go beyond providing temporary aid. While we support many community initiatives, our primary focus is supporting orphans, ensuring that they not only receive care and protection today but also have access to education, skills, and opportunities for a brighter future.

Instead of only offering financial support, we invest in sustainable community projects such as schools, farms, small businesses, and local initiatives. This way, the communities that orphans grow up in become stronger, more resilient, and self-sufficient.

We work hand in hand with local communities to develop solutions that truly matter. Whether it’s responding to urgent humanitarian needs or creating long-term strategic impact, our approach is always centered on people and their potential.

The Meaning Behind Our Name

The name Ummah Works reflects who we are and what we strive for. Ummah is the Arabic word for community, symbolizing unity, care, and shared responsibility. Works highlights that we are not just a foundation that gives. We build, create, and work alongside communities. Our goal is to establish sustainable projects with lasting impact, empowering the ummah to become self-sufficient without depending solely on financial aid.

Mission & Vision

Mission: Ummah Works Foundation is dedicated to supporting orphans by providing not just care and protection. We believe that lasting change starts with strong communities. That’s why our mission is to build communities, investing in education and local enterprise so that every child can grow up with the tools to create their own future and follow their goals.

Vision: A world where every orphan grows up in a supportive and self-reliant community, empowered to reach their full potential by accessing education and skills that match their aspirations and the world around them.
